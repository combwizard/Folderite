# Folderite

Folderite is a folder menu for Windows. Middle-click in Explorer or in an Open or Save dialog, and a menu of your favorites, the folders open in other windows, your recent folders and the folder on the clipboard appears at the cursor. Pick one and that window goes there. It is free, open source (MIT), and runs in the tray.
